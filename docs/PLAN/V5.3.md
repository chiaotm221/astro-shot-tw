# V5.3 Plan — Photo Sky and Trail Preview

## Phase 1 Delivery

- Screen-blend the locally rendered sky over the aligned imported photo.
- Provide star, Milky Way emphasis, and star-trail accumulation modes.
- Adjust overlay strength independently from photo opacity.
- Configure trail duration from 5–120 simulated minutes with common presets.
- Accumulate trail samples according to V3.2 simulation time, pause, and playback speed.
- Reset trail accumulation when its mode or duration changes.

## Rendering and Privacy

The preview copies the existing local sky canvas into a second browser canvas. Star trails use a `lighten` accumulation buffer, while the resulting layer uses CSS screen blending over the local photo. No photo pixels or preview frames are transmitted or persisted.

## Limitations

The output is a composition preview, not a calibrated exposure simulation. It inherits V5.2 manual alignment and does not claim plate-solved astrometric accuracy. Atmospheric conditions, lens distortion, sensor response, tracking errors, and foreground occlusion are not modeled.

## Follow-up

V5.4 will export confirmed plan data and a local preview while keeping external AI compositing separately consented.
