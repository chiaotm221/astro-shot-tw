# V5.2 Plan — Capture Confirmation and Alignment

## Phase 1 Delivery

- Confirm or correct EXIF GPS, capture time, elevation, focal length, and orientation.
- Label values as original EXIF or manual corrections without rewriting source metadata.
- Require manual azimuth and camera-tilt confirmation because common EXIF does not provide reliable values.
- Adjust imported-photo opacity, scale, and horizontal/vertical offset.
- Apply confirmed location and capture time to the simulator and smoothly align its view direction.
- Keep every adjustment reversible and leave the original local file untouched.

## Limitations

This is manual visual alignment, not astrometric plate solving. Device orientation tags describe pixel rotation, not the camera's compass direction. GPS and clock accuracy depend on the originating device and must be reviewed by the user.

## Follow-up

V5.3 will reuse this alignment state for star, Galactic Center, and trail overlays. Original EXIF provenance must remain distinct from corrected planning values.
