# V5.0 Plan — Camera and Composition Simulation

## Goal

Plan a night-sky composition using camera geometry without coupling the planner to physical camera control or capture processing.

## Phase 1 Delivery

- Select full-frame, APS-C, Micro Four Thirds, or representative phone-wide sensor dimensions.
- Set focal length from 8–200 mm with 14, 20, 24, 35, and 50 mm presets.
- Select 3:2, 16:9, or 4:3 framing and landscape or portrait orientation.
- Calculate horizontal and vertical angular field of view.
- Show an optional composition frame over the simulated sky with a rule-of-thirds grid.
- Adjust the frame vertically using a camera-tilt planning control.
- Guide the sky view to the Galactic Center and save up to six plans locally.

## Geometry

Field of view is calculated as `2 × atan(sensor dimension / (2 × focal length))`. Aspect-ratio crops are applied within the physical sensor dimensions before orientation is swapped. Phone dimensions are representative rather than tied to a specific device or lens-equivalence claim.

## Out of Scope

- Physical camera control or tethering.
- Lens distortion, nodal-point, focus, or depth-of-field modeling.
- Guaranteed landscape alignment without a user-confirmed camera direction and elevation.
- AI compositing or automatic replacement of captured skies.

## Follow-on Versions

- V5.1: local photo import, EXIF extraction, and explicit privacy messaging.
- V5.2: confirm GPS/time/direction and manually align the photo with the sky.
- V5.3: preview stars, the Milky Way, and star trails over the imported photo.
- V5.4: export the photography plan or explicitly opt into an external AI compositing workflow.
